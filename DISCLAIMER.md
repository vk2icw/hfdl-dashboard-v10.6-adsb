It is not an air traffic control system, certified aviation product, navigation aid, flight-safety system, emergency service, or authoritative source of aircraft information.

Received and decoded information may be incomplete, delayed, duplicated, incorrectly decoded, incorrectly associated with an aircraft, or based on outdated third-party metadata. Aircraft positions, identities, registrations, callsigns, operators, routes, altitudes and message contents must not be relied upon for navigation, safety-critical decisions, operational control, emergency response, legal evidence or commercial flight tracking.

A position shown on the map may represent an earlier HFDL position report rather than the aircraft's current location. Later non-position messages may keep an aircraft visible after the last valid position report.

Users must independently verify all information using appropriate authoritative sources.
